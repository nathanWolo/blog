## Projects

*   [crossfish](https://github.com/nathanWolo/crossfish)
    *   An Ultimate Tic Tac Toe Alpha-Beta engine in C++ with an NNUE evaluation. It was at one point the strongest UTTT engine, and remains one of the strongest.
*   [slinky](https://github.com/nathanWolo/slinky)
    *   A fast Battlesnake environment in JAX, used to train AlphaSlinky, one of the strongest BattleSnake agents.
*   [tronZero](https://github.com/nathanWolo/tron-codingame)
    *   tronzero, an AlphaZero-style agent for [CodinGame's Tron Battle](https://www.codingame.com/multiplayer/bot-programming/tron-battle) with two to four players. The game, the network's forward pass and the search are a dependency-free Rust engine, and training is done in JAX.
