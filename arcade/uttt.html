You are red. The big board is nine small games of tic-tac-toe. The cell you play in a small board decides which small board your opponent must play in next: play in a top-right cell, and they must play in the top-right board. If that board is already decided or full, they may play anywhere. The boards you may play in are tinted. Win three small boards in a row to win the game; if nobody manages that, the player who has won more small boards wins.

The opponent is crossfish, an alpha-beta engine with a small neural network for its evaluation. The engine here is the one written for CodinGame's arena, compiled to WebAssembly and running in your browser; the figures under the board are how many moves ahead it looked and how many positions it examined on its last move.
