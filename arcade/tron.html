You are red. Steer with the arrow keys or W A S D; on a phone, swipe on the board or use the buttons under it. Every bike leaves a wall behind it and moves one cell per turn, red first. Run into a wall and you lose. The dots show which bike can reach each cell first.

Both opponents come from tron-codingame. tronzero is the newer one: an AlphaZero-style agent, a small neural network guiding a tree search. alpha-beta is its predecessor, a classical search with a hand-written evaluation, and the engine that plays itself at the top of the home page. Each is compiled to WebAssembly and runs in your browser, thinking for most of every turn; the figure under the board is how many positions tronzero tried, or how many moves ahead alpha-beta looked, on its last move.
