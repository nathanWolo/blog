Arcade

Play against the game engines I have written.

Each engine here is the real one, compiled to WebAssembly. It runs in your browser, so nothing you do is sent to a server, and it thinks only as fast as your device lets it.

  • Tron Light cycles on a 30 by 20 grid. Do not hit a wall, and leave the other bike nowhere to go. Against tronzero or alpha-beta
  • Battlesnake Snake with opponents, on an 11 by 11 board. Outlast them, or meet them head-on when you are the longer one. Against AlphaSlinky
  • Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe Nine boards in one: each move decides where the other player goes next. Against crossfish
  • Poker No-limit hold'em sit-and-go, against one to three bots. Against the cgpoker bot
